# TUG//LAB — Operator Quick-Card

**One page. Operator-following, portrait camera.**

## Set up the course
- Sturdy **armchair** (seat ≈ 46 cm), **not against a wall**, won't slide/tip.
- Cone / floor marker **3 m (10 ft)** from the front of the chair.
- Participant: usual footwear, usual walking aid if needed, **no physical help**.
- Do **one untimed practice** trial first.

## Hold the phone
- **Portrait.** Keep the participant's **whole body (head to feet)** in frame.
- Stand to the side so you get a **side-on** view; pan **smoothly** to follow.
- Keep the walk **parallel to the camera** (don't let them drift toward/away).
- Good light; avoid strong backlight.

## Run the test (in the app)
1. Pick video language → watch → fill form → tick checks → **PROCEED**.
2. Allow camera. Wait for pre-flight checks to go green, then **START TEST**.
3. On **3-2-1-GO**, the participant stands, walks to the cone, turns, walks back,
   sits. **Follow them** with the phone the whole time.
4. Stay **beside** the participant for safety.
5. The app auto-finishes when they're seated and still. (Or tap **COMPLETE**.)

## After
- Check the **QC** badge. If **REVIEW**, read the flag tips and consider a retry.
- Tick any **CDC observations** you saw (slow pace, loss of balance, short
  strides, little arm swing, steadying on walls, shuffling, en-bloc turn, aid
  misuse).
- **SUBMIT** (uploads pseudonymous data if configured) and/or **DOWNLOAD ZIP**.
- Up to **2 attempts**; on the 2nd, choose **Keep previous** or **Use current**.

## Reading the result
- **Time ≥ 12 s → AT RISK** (CDC). Tiers: green <10, amber 10–12, red ≥12.
- It's a **screening estimate, not a diagnosis.** Refer on if concerned.

## If something's wrong
- "Camera error / needs HTTPS" → open the **GitHub Pages** URL, not a file.
- Body not fully in frame → step back or use the **0.5× wide** lens.
- Pose engine not loading → check internet (model loads from a CDN once).
- Never enter real names — **participant IDs only.**
